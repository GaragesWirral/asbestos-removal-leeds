What It Is

What Is an Asbestos Management Survey?

An asbestos management survey is the standard survey for a building in normal use. Its job is to find asbestos containing materials (ACMs) that could be damaged or disturbed during normal occupation, including routine maintenance and the installation of new equipment. It is the most common asbestos survey in Leeds for offices, shops, schools and rented buildings.

The surveyor inspects all reasonably accessible areas, which usually includes above suspended ceilings, inside risers and service cupboards, in roof spaces and in plant rooms. Suspect materials are sampled and sent to a UKAS accredited laboratory, or in some cases presumed to contain asbestos and recorded as such.

The result is an asbestos register: a record of every material found, where it is, what type of asbestos it contains, its condition and how likely it is to be disturbed. That register is the foundation of your asbestos management plan.

Who needs one

  • Owners and tenants of non domestic premises
  • Landlords and agents responsible for common parts
  • Managing agents of blocks of flats and HMOs
  • Schools, churches, community buildings and offices
  • Shops, warehouses, factories and workshops
  • Anyone whose lease gives them repair duties
The Law

The Duty to Manage Asbestos

Regulation 4 of the Control of Asbestos Regulations 2012 places a duty on whoever is responsible for maintaining or repairing non domestic premises. That person, the dutyholder, must:

  • Take reasonable steps to find out whether asbestos is present, and its amount, type and condition
  • Presume materials contain asbestos unless there is strong evidence they do not
  • Keep an up to date record of where asbestos is and what condition it is in
  • Assess the risk of anyone being exposed to fibres
  • Prepare a plan setting out how the risk will be managed, and put it into action
  • Review and monitor the plan, and give information to anyone who might disturb the asbestos

The duty applies to common parts of domestic buildings too, such as hallways, stairs, lift shafts, roof spaces and boiler rooms in blocks of flats and shared houses. A management survey is the simplest way to meet the first steps of the duty, and it gives you the information the rest depends on.

The Report

What You Get From Your Asbestos Survey

Your asbestos report is written to be used, not filed away. It includes:

  • An executive summary with any urgent actions first
  • The asbestos register, room by room, with photographs
  • Lab results for every sample taken
  • A material and priority risk assessment for each item
  • Clear recommendations: manage, label, repair, encapsulate or remove
  • A list of any areas not accessed, so nothing is assumed

After the survey

Most asbestos found in a management survey can stay where it is, as long as it is in good condition, labelled where appropriate and checked regularly. Damaged items may need repair, sealing or asbestos removal, and we can help with any of those.

Asbestos in your register should be reinspected regularly so you notice if its condition changes. For most premises that means at least once a year. We can carry out reinspections and update your register.

Before building work

A management survey is not enough before refurbishment or demolition. If you are planning work that will disturb the fabric of the building, you also need a refurbishment and demolition survey of the affected areas.

How It Works

How an Asbestos Survey in Leeds Runs

We start by asking about the building: its age, size, use and any previous asbestos information you hold. That lets us give a fixed price and plan the visit around your occupants.

Management surveys are designed to cause as little disruption as possible. The building can stay in use, and sample points are sealed after sampling. For schools, offices and shops we can work early, late or at weekends.

Samples go to a UKAS accredited laboratory, and the report follows once results are back. If you manage several properties across Leeds and West Yorkshire, we can survey them as a programme and give you a consistent register for each.

Your asbestos management plan

The survey tells you what is there. The management plan sets out what you will do about it: who is responsible, how each item will be managed, how contractors will be told before they start work, and when the next reinspection is due.

We can help you write a practical asbestos management plan from your survey results, and keep it under review as the building changes.
