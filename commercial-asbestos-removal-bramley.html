Asbestos Materials Most Common in Bramley's Commercial Buildings

Bramley's commercial building stock is dominated by Victorian and Edwardian properties along Town Street and the older shopping centre units, alongside 1950s–70s industrial and workshop buildings near the canal. Because Yorkshire Asbestos Solutions operates from Bramley, we know exactly what tends to be found in each building era here.

In the older Victorian commercial properties, terraced shopfronts, converted warehouses, and former wool industry buildings, the most common finds are artex and textured coatings applied during 1960s–80s renovations, vinyl floor tiles with asbestos adhesive under more recent flooring, and pipe lagging in cellar and service areas. In the post-war workshop and light industrial units on the canal corridor, asbestos cement roofing sheets and asbestos insulation board (AIB) in welfare area ceilings and partition walls are by far the most frequent finds. Former Bramley Baths-era civic buildings may also contain sprayed asbestos coatings on structural steelwork, a feature typical of public buildings built between the 1930s and 1960s.
