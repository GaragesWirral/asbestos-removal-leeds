Emergency Asbestos Removal in Leeds

When asbestos-containing materials are accidentally damaged or disturbed, fibres can be released into the air posing an immediate health risk to occupants. Common emergency situations include drilling into asbestos insulation board, ceiling collapses exposing artex, storm damage to asbestos garage roofs, and accidental damage during unplanned building work.

Yorkshire Asbestos Solutions provides emergency asbestos removal across Leeds and West Yorkshire. Call us on 0113 519 9653 and we will assess the situation, advise on immediate safety steps, and deploy a licensed team as quickly as possible.

What to Do If Asbestos Is Disturbed

If you suspect asbestos has been disturbed, stop work immediately and keep people away from the area. Do not sweep, vacuum, or attempt to clean up dust yourself, this spreads fibres further. Close doors to isolate the area if safe to do so, and ventilate the rest of the property without drawing air through the contaminated space.

Call our emergency line and describe what happened, what material was involved, and where it is located. We will guide you through immediate safety measures while arranging a rapid site visit. The faster the response, the lower the risk of fibre spread throughout the property.

Our Emergency Response Process

Our emergency asbestos removal team operates across all Leeds districts including Harehills, Beeston, Hunslet, Seacroft, and the wider West Yorkshire area. On arrival, we assess the extent of contamination, establish a controlled work zone, and carry out safe removal or sealing of damaged materials.

Air monitoring may be conducted to confirm fibre levels before reoccupation. We provide full documentation of the emergency response including waste consignment notes and clearance certification. Our goal is to make the property safe as quickly as possible while maintaining full HSE compliance.

Preventing Future Emergencies

Many asbestos emergencies happen because no survey was carried out before building work began. A refurbishment or demolition survey before any structural work would identify ACMs and allow planned, controlled removal rather than an emergency callout.

If you are a landlord, contractor, or homeowner planning work on a pre-2000 property in Leeds, book an asbestos survey before you start. It is far cheaper and safer than dealing with an emergency.
