Emergency Asbestos Removal When Asbestos Is Damaged

Drilled into a board or cracked an asbestos garage roof? When asbestos is damaged or disturbed, fibres can get into the air and create real health risks for everyone in the building, in homes and commercial premises alike. Common emergencies include drilling into asbestos insulation board, storm damage to roof sheets, a broken artex ceiling, damaged pipe lagging, and builders who find asbestos halfway through a renovation.

Call our emergency line on 0113 519 9653. We will tell you what to do now and send a qualified asbestos team with a same day response.

What to Do If Asbestos Is Disturbed

Stop work immediately and keep everyone away from the area. Do not sweep, vacuum or wipe up dust yourself, as this spreads asbestos fibres further. As a precaution, close doors to isolate the room, turn off heating or air systems that move air around.

When you call, tell us what happened and which material was involved. A builder or homeowner often cannot be sure what the material is, so we can arrange sampling while we plan containment and the site visit.

Specialist Emergency Response Across West Yorkshire

On arrival our team checks the condition of asbestos and how far contamination has spread, sets up controls and removes the damaged material and debris. The aim is to stop asbestos exposure quickly, because people who inhale fibres can develop asbestosis, lung cancer or mesothelioma years later.

Air monitoring can confirm fibre levels before the area is used again. All hazardous waste is double bagged, and we transport and dispose of asbestos at a licensed waste disposal site. You receive the paperwork, including waste consignment notes and a clearance certificate where needed, so the job is safe and compliant.

Preventing the Next Emergency With a Survey

Most emergencies happen because nobody carried out an asbestos survey before the work began. A refurbishment or demolition survey finds asbestos first, so professional asbestos removal can be planned.

If you are a landlord, contractor or homeowner planning work on a pre 2000 property, book a survey before you start. For materials in good condition we can also help you manage asbestos in place with encapsulation, alongside our planned removal services and asbestos disposal.
