Local Knowledge

Where Is Asbestos Commonly Found in Wakefield Properties?

Wakefield has a significant proportion of housing built during the post-war period through to the late 1980s — exactly the era when asbestos-containing materials were in widespread use across the construction industry. From terrace houses in Castleford to industrial units in Pontefract, ACMs are a common find in the district.

The most frequently encountered materials in Wakefield asbestos surveys include:

  • Asbestos cement garage and outbuilding roofs — extremely common across the district
  • Artex and textured ceiling coatings applied pre-1985
  • Floor tiles and adhesive backing in kitchens and hallways
  • Pipe lagging on boilers, hot water cylinders, and central heating pipes
  • Asbestos insulation board (AIB) in partition walls, ceiling tiles, and soffits
  • Roof felts and guttering on older commercial and industrial premises
  • Fireplace surrounds and hearth panels in pre-1980s homes
