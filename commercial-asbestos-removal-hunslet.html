Asbestos Materials Found in Hunslet's Industrial and Commercial Buildings

As the "Workshop of Leeds," Hunslet contains some of the most asbestos-intensive commercial and industrial premises in the entire Leeds district. Decades of heavy industrial use, locomotives, brewing, foundries, textiles, have left a building stock where multiple types of asbestos were used in the original construction and in subsequent upgrades across the 20th century.

Large-scale industrial and factory buildings in Hunslet LS10 most commonly contain sprayed asbestos coatings on structural steelwork, used extensively in factories and plant rooms built or upgraded between the 1940s and 1970s. Asbestos cement roofing and cladding covers the majority of older industrial buildings on the trading estate. AIB ceiling tiles and fire protection boards are found in the office, welfare, and distribution areas of post-war commercial premises. The art deco industrial buildings from the 1930s, such as the former Tetley Brewery complex, may contain asbestos rope seals, gaskets, and lagging in plant rooms, materials that were standard in large brewing and processing facilities.
