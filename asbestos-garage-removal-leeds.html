Asbestos Garage Removal in Leeds

Asbestos cement garages are one of the most common asbestos-containing structures found in Leeds gardens. Millions were installed across the UK between the 1930s and 1980s, and the corrugated asbestos cement sheets used for roofs and wall panels remain in properties throughout Bramley, Pudsey, Morley, Farsley, and virtually every Leeds suburb.

When an asbestos garage becomes damaged, leaking, or simply needs replacing, professional removal is essential. Yorkshire Asbestos Solutions provides complete asbestos garage removal, including roof sheets, wall panels, and the full structure, carried out safely by HSE-licensed operatives.

Roof-Only vs Full Garage Dismantle

Some clients need only the asbestos roof sheets removed, allowing them to re-roof with modern materials while retaining the existing brick or block walls. Others require the entire garage dismantled and removed from site. We provide both services and will advise on the most cost-effective approach during our free site visit.

Damaged or weathered asbestos cement sheets are particularly hazardous as they are more likely to release fibres. If your garage roof is crumbling, cracked, or has been disturbed by storms, do not attempt to remove it yourself. Our team uses controlled wet removal techniques and full PPE to handle the material safely.

What the Removal Process Involves

We begin with a site assessment to check access, proximity to boundaries, and the condition of the asbestos sheets. On the day of removal, the work area is cordoned off and our operatives carefully remove each sheet, keeping them intact where possible to minimise fibre release.

All asbestos waste is wrapped, labelled, and transported to a licensed hazardous waste facility. We issue a consignment note confirming legal disposal. The site is left clean and ready for your builder to install a replacement structure or for the area to be repurposed.

How Much Does Asbestos Garage Removal Cost?

Costs depend on the size of the garage, accessibility, and whether you need a roof-only or full dismantle. A standard single garage roof removal typically starts from around £450, with full garage dismantles costing more depending on size and access. We provide fixed-price quotes after a free site visit, no hidden fees.
