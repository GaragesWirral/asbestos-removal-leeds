Local Knowledge

Asbestos in Castleford Properties

Postwar estates such as Airedale and Ferry Fryston were built quickly using fire resistant materials that often contained asbestos. Cement soffits, fascias, garages and sheds are common, and inside we often find textured ceilings, floor tiles and insulation board in heater cupboards.

Older terraced streets near the town centre were mostly modernised in the 1960s and 70s, bringing the same materials indoors. Industrial and commercial buildings across WF10 often have cement roof sheets, and older units can have lagging in plant rooms.

These are the asbestos containing materials our surveys find most often:

  • Textured coatings on ceilings and walls
  • Floor tiles and bitumen adhesive
  • Asbestos insulation board in cupboards, soffits and ceilings
  • Cement garage roofs, gutters and flues
  • Pipe lagging and boiler insulation
  • Roof sheets and cladding on commercial buildings

Do you legally need a survey?

If you are responsible for non domestic premises in Castleford, including the common parts of flats and shared houses, the Control of Asbestos Regulations 2012 give you a duty to manage asbestos. A management survey is the usual starting point.

Before any refurbishment or demolition of a building built before 2000, a refurbishment and demolition survey of the affected areas is needed so builders know what they are working with. Homeowners are not legally required to survey a home they live in, but a survey before building work or a purchase is sensible.

How It Works

What Happens on a Castleford Asbestos Survey

Booking. We ask about the building, its age and what you plan to do, then confirm the survey type, scope and a fixed price in writing.

The inspection. A surveyor inspects every area in scope, takes small samples of suspect materials and seals each sample point. For a refurbishment survey the rooms usually need to be empty because the inspection is intrusive.

The lab. Samples go to a UKAS accredited laboratory for analysis.

The report. You receive an asbestos report with a register of everything found, photographs, the lab results, a risk assessment and clear recommendations for each item. If removal is needed, we can quote for it, but you are free to use any licensed contractor.

We are regularly working in and around Castleford, so we can usually book a survey in at short notice.

Choosing an asbestos surveyor in Castleford

Whoever carries out your asbestos survey in Castleford, ask whether their asbestos surveyors hold the BOHS P402 qualification or equivalent, whether the surveying company is UKAS accredited, and which laboratory carries out the asbestos sampling and testing. The HSE recommends using an accredited surveying organisation.
