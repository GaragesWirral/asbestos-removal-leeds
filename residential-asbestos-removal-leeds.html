Professional Residential Asbestos Removal in Leeds

Many homes across Leeds and West Yorkshire still contain asbestos-containing materials (ACMs). From Victorian terraces in Harehills and Chapel Allerton to post-war semis in Seacroft and Roundhay, asbestos was used extensively in British housing until it was banned in 1999. If you are planning a renovation, extension, or simply suspect asbestos in your property, professional residential asbestos removal is the only safe option.

At Yorkshire Asbestos Solutions, we specialise in domestic asbestos removal for homeowners, landlords, and property managers. Based in Bramley, we are your local contractor, not a national call centre. We understand Leeds housing stock and the specific ACMs commonly found in homes across the city.

Common Asbestos Materials Found in Leeds Homes

Residential properties in Leeds frequently contain a range of asbestos materials. Artex and textured ceiling coatings are among the most common, particularly in homes renovated between the 1960s and 1980s. Vinyl floor tiles, asbestos cement garage roofs, pipe lagging, soffits, and insulation board are also regularly encountered in properties across Bramley, Armley, Kirkstall, and surrounding areas.

The age and type of your property largely determines what ACMs may be present. Pre-1960 terraces in areas like Wortley and Beeston often contain artex and floor tiles, while post-war estates in Moortown and Roundhay may have asbestos insulation board in ceiling voids and garage structures. A survey before any work is always recommended.

Our Residential Removal Process

Every residential asbestos removal job follows a strict HSE-compliant process. We begin with a site assessment to identify the scope of work and any access considerations. Our licensed operatives then establish a controlled work area using polythene enclosures and negative air pressure units where required.

Materials are carefully removed using wet suppression techniques to minimise fibre release. All asbestos waste is double-bagged, labelled, and transported to a licensed disposal facility. We provide a waste consignment note and clearance certificate on completion, giving you documented proof that the work was carried out to the highest standard.

When Do You Need Residential Asbestos Removal?

You may need residential asbestos removal if you are renovating a kitchen or bathroom, converting a loft, removing a garage, replacing old floor tiles, or if asbestos-containing materials have been damaged or disturbed. Landlords with HMOs and rental properties also have a legal duty to manage asbestos under the Control of Asbestos Regulations.

If you are buying or selling a property and a survey has flagged asbestos, we can assess the materials and advise whether removal, encapsulation, or ongoing management is the most appropriate course of action. Never attempt DIY removal, breaking asbestos materials releases microscopic fibres that pose serious long-term health risks.
