Asbestos Garages in Bramley, Why Post-War Estates Drive High Demand

Bramley's large post-war council estates, on Hough Lane, Rossefield, and the Ridge, were built during the peak era of asbestos cement use in UK housebuilding. Almost every garage block on these estates used corrugated asbestos cement sheets for roofing, and many used asbestos panels for side walls too. Because Yorkshire Asbestos Solutions is based in Bramley, we work on these streets every week and know the exact specification of garages built in each estate era, which means accurate estimates and no wasted site visits.

Beyond the council estates, Victorian and Edwardian terraces along Town Street and Stanningley Road often had asbestos cement garage roofs added during 20th-century improvements. These are generally smaller, single-skin structures in rear yards, and they carry the same removal requirements as the estate blocks, regardless of size.

Roof Sheets Only, or the Whole Structure?

Many Bramley homeowners who have retained sound brick or block walls simply need the asbestos cement roof removed so a builder can re-roof with modern materials. We remove the sheets cleanly, leaving the walls and base ready for the next phase. Where the whole structure needs to come down, roof, walls, base clearance, we provide that as a single complete job.

Cracked, crumbling, or weathered asbestos sheets are the most urgent cases. If the roof on your Bramley garage has deteriorated, each period of wet or windy weather increases the risk of fibre release. We can usually attend within 24–48 hours of a free assessment for sheets in poor condition.
