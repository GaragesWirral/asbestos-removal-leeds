Local Knowledge

Where Asbestos Turns Up in Batley Properties

Postwar homes in Batley and Birstall often have cement soffits, garage roofs and rainwater goods, plus textured ceilings and floor tiles inside. Older terraces were usually updated in the 1960s and 70s, bringing in asbestos boards around boilers and in cupboards.

Commercial and industrial buildings are a big part of our work here. Warehouses and units built before 2000 frequently have cement roof sheets and cladding, and older mills can have lagging and insulation board in plant areas. Owners of those buildings have a legal duty to find and manage asbestos.

The materials we find most often in and around Batley are:

  • Cement soffits, fascias and downpipes
  • Garage and outbuilding roofs
  • Textured ceilings
  • Floor tiles and adhesives
  • Insulation board in cupboards and around heating
  • Roof sheets and cladding on warehouses and industrial units

Licensed or non licensed work?

The law splits asbestos jobs into licensed and non licensed work. Sprayed coatings, lagging and most insulation board sit at the top of the risk scale, so the HSE requires a licensed contractor, advance notification and a four stage clearance at the end.

Cement products, which make up most garage roofs and old drainpipes, are normally non licensed. The Control of Asbestos Regulations still apply, so the work has to be planned, done wet where possible, wrapped and taken to a site permitted to accept it. We explain the category in writing with every quote.

Why It Matters

Why Asbestos in Batley Homes Needs Care

Asbestos was used in thousands of building products across West Yorkshire until it was fully banned in the UK in 1999. The three main types of asbestos found in buildings are chrysotile (white), amosite (brown) and crocidolite (blue). All of them are dangerous when fibres are released into the air and breathed in.

Those fibres can cause serious diseases, including mesothelioma, lung cancer and asbestosis, often decades after the exposure. That is why asbestos containing materials that are in good condition and left alone are usually low risk, while drilling, sanding or breaking them is not.

A qualified asbestos surveyor can tell you what is present and in what condition. Our asbestos survey and testing service covers Batley and the rest of West Yorkshire, and every sample goes to a UKAS accredited laboratory. Once you know what you have, you can decide calmly whether it needs removal, encapsulation or simply keeping an eye on.

Asbestos Waste

Getting Rid of Asbestos Waste in Batley

Batley is in Kirklees, and the council takes household asbestos at the Emerald Street household waste recycling centre in Huddersfield. You must book in first through the council's booking system, and the service is for householders only.

Trade waste is not accepted. If you are a landlord or business, or a builder is doing the work, the asbestos has to go through a licensed carrier to a permitted site.

We can take the whole job off your hands: removal, wrapping, transport and disposal, with the consignment note at the end. Check the Kirklees Council asbestos page for current details.
