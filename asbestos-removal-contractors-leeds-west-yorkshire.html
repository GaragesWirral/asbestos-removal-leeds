Regulatory Compliance

What Makes a Qualified Asbestos Removal Contractor?

The law is clear on what qualifications and processes are required from licensed asbestos removal contractors in the UK. Here's what we adhere to on every single job.

📜

HSE Licence

A current Health and Safety Executive (HSE) licence is a legal requirement for all high-risk asbestos work in the UK. Our licence is renewed regularly and covers all licensable asbestos activities including AIB, sprayed coatings, and pipe lagging removal.

📋

Written Plan of Work

Under the Control of Asbestos Regulations 2012, every licensed job must have a written plan of work before starting. This covers the scope of removal, containment methods, PPE, and emergency procedures. We provide this on every project as standard.

👷

Trained & Certificated Operatives

All of our asbestos operatives hold recognised qualifications including City & Guilds or equivalent asbestos-related certificates. Annual refresher training and health surveillance ensure our team is always working to the highest standards.

🌬️

Air Monitoring & Clearance Testing

During all licensed asbestos work, continuous air monitoring is required to ensure fibre levels remain safe. Once removal is complete, a final air clearance test is conducted and a clearance certificate is issued — your legal confirmation that the area is safe.

🚛

Licensed Hazardous Waste Disposal

All asbestos waste is classified as hazardous and must be disposed of at a licensed facility by a registered hazardous waste carrier. We handle all asbestos waste disposal and provide you with a waste transfer note — your legal proof of compliant disposal.

🔔

HSE Notification (Licensed Work)

All licensed asbestos work must be notified to the HSE at least 14 days before starting. We handle all notifications on your behalf, ensuring you are fully protected and that the job proceeds within the law from day one.
